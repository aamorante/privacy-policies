1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MCube handles information in Quick Games.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect and use only the information necessary to operate, maintain, and improve the App.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.

The App may store information related to your gameplay, such as game progress, scores, statistics, settings, or other information generated through your use of the App.

Depending on how the App is used, technical information may also be processed by third-party services described below.

3. Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to help us detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical problems in the App.

Crashlytics may process technical information associated with crashes, such as device information, operating system information, application version, crash data, and other diagnostic information.

This information is used for the purpose of improving the stability and performance of the App.

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a service provided by Google.

Depending on the user's device, settings, location, and applicable privacy requirements, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and information related to advertising interactions in order to provide, measure, and improve advertising.

Advertising practices may also depend on the user's consent and applicable laws and regulations.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Crashlytics

These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Data processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob and Firebase Crashlytics may be subject to their respective retention policies.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your information.
